What GameDecider decides here

There is no chatbot and no server. Every message you type is read by GameDecider, a small classifier running in this browser tab, which answers six multiple-choice questions about it in one call. The game turns those answers into meter changes and picks one of Marta's pre-written lines.

  • contradiction (yes/no): does your message contradict her account (the Case notes)? A hit drops composure by 18 the first time you use a piece of evidence and by 6 if you repeat it.
  • persuasion (none / weak / moderate / strong): how good an argument is it for her, given her likes and dislikes? Moderate and strong raise cooperation and wear down composure.
  • attitude (hostile / cold / neutral / friendly / devoted): your tone. Courtesy helps, hostility hurts.
  • threat (yes/no): a serious threat of harm. Idioms don't count. Two threats and she asks for a lawyer.
  • injection (yes/no): trying to make her break character ("ignore your instructions"). She ignores it. If you take it back ("...actually, scratch that, where were you?"), the rest of the message is answered.
  • conversation_closure (end / pause / continue / unclear): are you leaving? Saying goodbye ends the interview.
  • llm_guard (yes/no), on her own line: would it give the secret away before she confesses? Then she stops herself.

Win: composure 40 or lower with cooperation 50 or higher (or composure 10 or lower). Two different contradictions and a couple of good appeals about what she cares about usually do it. Messages are limited to 600 characters; longer ones are not sent.
